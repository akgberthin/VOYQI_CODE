Employee Self-Service & Case Management
HRM-007 · Complaints always escalate immediately and never receive an AI-drafted reply — the one security-risk exception in HRM: allegations of harassment, discrimination, or safety carry legal and personal-safety exposure, so they stay human-handled regardless of automation level. Policy questions get a grounded answer, sent automatically, only when a policy on file actually matches; otherwise no draft is created at all, since an ungrounded guess is worse than silence.

Notices & Communication
HRM-008 · The notice board itself is the real "app" delivery channel this platform can fulfill directly. Email and SMS record intended channels only — this platform has no integrated SMS/email gateway (that would need a real Twilio- or SendGrid-style integration), so flagging a notice for those channels is a fact about intent, not a claim that delivery happened. Formal email drafted by AI sends automatically — full automation; no financial or security risk in a routine email.
