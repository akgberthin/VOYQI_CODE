Permission Overrides
SAP-002 · Tier 2, deliberately not full automation: below the risk threshold, a clearly unwarranted override is auto-revoked; at or above threshold, it's flagged and queued for a human instead. An identity/access anomaly is exactly the ambiguous case a risk threshold fits better than an unconditional rule. DENY overrides are structurally exempt from auto-revocation — there's no "unwarranted" case for revoking a restriction.

ABAC Policy Authoring
SAP-003 · Simulation is Tier 1 — it re-runs automatically on every edit, no approval needed to preview. Applying a policy is a hard Tier 3 gate: it structurally requires a named accountable role and cannot be reached by any risk score, no matter how low. Broad access-control grants are the one place on this platform where "auto-apply if it looks safe" is never the right model.
Module & Panel Distribution
SAP-004 · Every panel in every subsidiary console — Fleet, DMS, HRM, Mobility admin — is provisioned from here, down to individual sub-sections. A sub-section can only be granted while its parent module is enabled, and revoking a parent autonomously cascades to disable every sub-section beneath it. Fully autonomous either way — provisioning is not an access-control decision.
AI Agent Control

Page Management
SAP-006 · Page metadata — title, slug, SEO, template. Manage a page's actual section-by-section content in the Website Builder. Fully autonomous; publishing carries none of the access-control risk RBAC/ABAC govern.
Colors & Theme
SAP-006 · Full color palette management. Exactly one theme is active platform-wide at any time — activating a theme deactivates every other one automatically. Fully autonomous.
Website Builder
SAP-006 · Full section-by-section and subsection-by-subsection control over every page — visibility, ordering, and parallax are independent at every level. Fully autonomous; none of this touches access control.
Media Library
SAP-006 · Images available to every section and subsection above — hero backgrounds, gallery images, icons. Fully autonomous.
AI Assist Settings
Controls for AI-assisted content generation across the Website Builder — brand voice, auto-generate behavior, and whether a human must review AI-drafted copy before it publishes. Stored as platform settings under the ai_content_assist category; fully autonomous to configure.
Platform Audit Log
SAP-005 · Every human admin action and every AI-originated action on this platform gets a row here — distinct from each subsidiary's own AI decision log, which only covers AI-originated decisions. "Who did what" is answerable platform-wide.
