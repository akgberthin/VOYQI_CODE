Incident & Claims Management
FLT-005 · Liability is always a human call, recorded exactly as entered — the AI has no path to that field, structurally, and never did. Fully autonomous for its own output: every reported incident gets an AI severity triage, and SEVERE immediately opens a repair order — no human has to notice and start it.

Floorplan Financing
FLT-008/009 · Facilities and per-VIN collateral pledges are plain facts — VIN collateral status is never AI-judged. Fully autonomous: every pledge gets an AI covenant-risk read, and a BREACHED verdict immediately freezes the facility — a real block on further pledges, not just a warning. Unfreezing stays the one deliberate human-only action, since clearing a lender covenant breach is an attestation a human needs to make.
